On the logo

Use the mark in the bar, keep the lockup for covers

The stacked logo is a vertical lockup, and an app bar is a horizontal space — fitting one inside the other is what forces today's header to 96px and a 78px logo column. The wordmark is also a hand-drawn display face rendered as a raster: at bar size its letters are about 8px tall and go soft, and it can't pick up currentColor in dark mode the way type can.

So: crop the meerkat out as its own asset and set the name in type beside it, as in 2a. The full lockup still earns its place where there is vertical room and no navigation to share it with — a PDF cover page, the docs site, the README, the favicon. Same brand, two lockups, which is normal practice rather than a compromise.
